RULE 3 / SITE IDENTITY: made.com/nl/ is NOT a live Made.com catalogue. It is a chain of three 301 redirects, fully confirmed via curl_cffi (safari15_3) with allow_redirects and history capture: https://www.made.com/nl/ -> 301 -> https://www.nextdirect.com/nl/nl/made -> 301 -> https://www.nextdirect.com/nl/nl/shop/brand-madecom -> 301 -> https://www.nextdirect.com/nl/nl/brands/madecom (200, final). This is a materially DIFFERENT and MORE DIMINISHED situation than S.N. 51 Made.com UK (read first, per assignment): UK stayed on the made.com domain and runs its own Next-operated storefront with real department URLs (/shop/lighting, /shop/tableware, etc, 1758 products total). NL leaves the made.com domain entirely and lands on nextdirect.com - Next's general international multi-category retail site (clothing, homeware, everything) - where 'Made' exists only as a BRAND FILTER FACET (brand:madecom) inside Next's broader Homeware department, not as any kind of dedicated storefront or category tree. Page <title> is literally 'MADE | Next Official Site', meta description 'Shop for MADE at Next.' SITE LEGITIMACY: nextdirect.com is genuinely Next's own platform (image assets served from cms.platform.next, the same Next CMS infrastructure identified at made.com UK); no closing-down-sale language, no scam indicators - this is Next Retail's real international storefront, just with Made reduced to a brand tag. CATALOGUE SIZE: the entire brand:madecom facet totals only 94 products SITE-WIDE (all under the single department:homeware facet value - no lighting, no furniture-as-separate-dept even appears), independently confirmed via the embedded search JSON (search.totalResults:94) and via the facets.department:homeware.c:94 count on https://www.nextdirect.com/nl/nl/brands/madecom. Of those 94, the category facet breaks out as: cushions 25, bedsets 20, dinnerware 8, curtains 7, wallart 5, glasses 4, towels 4, vases 4, pillowcases 4, throws 3, bedsheets 2, bathmats 2, bowls 2, plantpots 1 (facet counts do not sum to 94 exactly because some products carry no category tag or multiple tags - a facet-list artifact, not a count error). CRITICALLY: no Lighting category, no Mirrors category and no Clocks category exist anywhere in this brand facet (Made UK by contrast had 396 Lighting rows + 32 Mirrors + 7 Clocks) - confirming this is not a genuinely separate Dutch catalogue but a residual, near-liquidation-scale tail of Made SKUs folded into Next's general range.

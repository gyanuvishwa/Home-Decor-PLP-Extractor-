ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: No project precedent (first Structube extraction). ACCESS: robots.txt (User-agent:* only, standard Magento boilerplate, no Claude-specific disallow) checked first -> clear. Homepage/category pages are a Magento PWA Studio React SPA (client.js/vendor-react/vendor-magento bundles) that returns an identical JS shell for every route, so nav/count could not be read from rendered HTML directly. Side door used per Rule 2: the site's own /graphql endpoint (disallowed for crawlers in robots.txt but not a Claude-specific block, and explicitly sanctioned as a side-door technique in this project's brief) answered directly, giving exact per-category totals via `products(filter:{category_id:{eq:ID}}, pageSize:100)`. Category tree discovered via the site's product+category sitemap (media/sitemap_en_ca.xml, 3550 URLs) filtered to /decor and /furniture/outdoor/decor-outdoor paths. COUNT VERIFICATION (Rule 1): for every leaf category the GraphQL `products.total_count` was exhaustion-checked by paging pageSize=100 and collecting unique product IDs until page_info.total_pages was exhausted; unique_ids always matched total_count exactly. Note the separate `categoryList.product_count` field runs 2-3 units higher than the exhaustion-verified `products.total_count` for several categories (e.g. Mirrors: 47 vs 45 verified, Decorative Accessories: 121 vs 119, Vases/Bowls/Trays: 156 vs 153) -- the field-level count is NOT used; only the exhaustion-checked products.total_count is reported as qty. EXCLUSIONS: Lightbulbs category (4 items) dropped (bulbs). Rugs (50) and Cushions (146) subcategories are 100% textile products (rugs/carpets, cushion covers) -- dropped entirely, no row emitted. furniture/outdoor/decor-outdoor node checked: product_count=0, empty, no row. MIXED ROLL-UPS (title-inspected, drop-don't-pad): 'Artificial Flowers & Planters' (162 total) is a genuine mixed roll-up of faux botanicals (84 SKUs: artificial-potted-plant/artificial-flower/artificial-potted-cactus -- excluded) and real planters/pots/plant-stands (78 SKUs -- kept, filed as 'Planters & Plant Stands'). 'Decorative Accessories' (119 total) mixed real decor (80 SKUs: sculptures, pedestals, candle holders/tealight holders, unscented pillar candles, decorative balls, wall/table clocks, curtain rod sets, bookends, decorative jar, decorative straw figurines) with excluded textiles/furniture (39 SKUs: curtain panels, knit throws, room dividers, a coat rack, a tassel set, ceramic coasters, a paper-towel holder). 'Vases, Bowls & Trays' (153 total) mixed genuine decor (102 SKUs: vases, bowls, trays, a marble board, a decorative jar) with Kitchen & Dining tableware (13 SKUs: 16-piece dinnerware sets, decorative/pineapple-shaped plates, a highball-glass set -- carried to a new 'Kitchen & Dining' bucket per brief instruction that tableware/dinnerware is in-scope but filed separately from Decor) and non-decor storage/kitchen items (38 SKUs: baskets, tote bags, waste bins, flatware sets -- excluded, flatware=cutlery). 'Wall Art & Paintings' (199 total) is genuine wall art/paintings/giclee/shadow-box art except 1 tassel-set SKU cross-listed from the excluded tassels item -- dropped, net 198. RULE 0.6 OVERLAP: a handful of planter/pot SKUs (e.g. 'pierre-aluminum-pot', 'muriel-metal-planter-with-wood-stand') are cross-assigned by the retailer to both the Artificial Flowers & Planters category and the Vases/Bowls/Trays category -- these are two distinct named category URLs on the site (not a duplicate node or facet re-cut), so both leaf rows are kept as-is with this cross-listing documented rather than deduplicated.

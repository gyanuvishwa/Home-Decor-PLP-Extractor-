They sit under the site's Trees and Plants menus but are their own listings; the two collections were checked for overlap and share ZERO products. CANDLES: included only after confirming they are unscented decorative taper and pillar candles (no scent/fragrance/aroma wording on the PDPs); Bloomr has no home-fragrance range. EXCLUSIONS INSIDE OTHERWISE IN-SCOPE MENUS: 'Decorative Boxes & Jewelry Boxes' (145) dropped - all 144 sampled titles across 3 pages are jewelry boxes and the PDP copy sells them as 'practical storage' to 'keep your treasured pieces organized', so the category is storage-led; 'Baskets & Rattan' (14) dropped - water-hyacinth storage baskets sold to 'keep your space clutter-free... neatly out of sight'; Cushion Covers (1,012), Throws (51), Placemats and Napkins & Napkin Holders dropped as textiles; Cutlery (25) dropped as flatware; Bulbs (9) dropped as lighting hardware; Bathroom Accessories (8) dropped. Also excluded wholesale: all Furniture, Bedlinen & Towels, Rugs & Carpets, Christmas/ornaments, and Sale/Outlet/Best-Seller promo collections.

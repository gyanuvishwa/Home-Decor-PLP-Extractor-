ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: SITE IDENTITY (Rule 3): aerin.com is AERIN's own Magento 2 / Adobe Commerce storefront and it is a fully shoppable catalogue (1,622 products indexed), not a lookbook. It carries BOTH the Estee Lauder AERIN beauty line and the AERIN lifestyle/home business on one domain. The roster URL https://www.aerin.com/home-decor is not a real path; the home decor tree is /home/ -> /home/decor/ and /home/tabletop-bar/, plus a separate top-level /lighting/. ACCESS: every direct route was HTTP 403 from PerimeterX behind Fastly - all 11 curl_cffi impersonation profiles (desktop and mobile), Googlebot / Bingbot / Screaming Frog UAs, translate.goog, WebFetch, /graphql, /rest/V1 and the sitemap; r.jina.ai was itself Cloudflare-challenged for this workstation across 12 retries; microlink's headless renderer was blocked too. Only /robots.txt was allowlisted. Two routes worked: (1) the api.allorigins.win server-side fetch proxy, which returned the genuine 281 KB category HTML and with it the full snowdogMenu mega-menu (every category URL below is taken verbatim from that nav) plus the Klevu config; (2) the storefront's own Klevu search layer on uscs32v2.ksearchnet.com, which is not behind PerimeterX. COUNTS: the category pages render their grid and their #toolbar-amount header client-side from Klevu, so the Klevu CATNAV totalResultsFound for a categoryPath IS the number the site reports. Every count was verified empirically (Rule 1): enumerated to exhaustion at 100 per page, unique product-id count equals the reported total, records still returned at offset=total-1 and zero at offset=total, on all 20 categories tested. Cross-checked against an independent full-catalogue dump (1,622 records / 1,622 unique ids): CATNAV matched the denormalised klevu_category tally exactly on 19 of 20 leaves and on every parent node (Home 687, Home;Decor 388, Home;Tabletop & Bar 328, Lighting 764, Ceiling Lights 360, Lamps 185, Wall Lights 207). Only Barware diverged (82 live vs 94 stale) and the live CATNAV figure was used. DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED: all Beauty (fragrance, scent, skincare, brushes, makeup bags), Fashion, Gifts, The Edit, Collaborations, New Arrivals, Sale, Bestsellers, Heirloom Collection, Personalize, Antique & Vintage, Living with Flowers, and the Lighting > Style / Collection filter pages. SUBSET CHECK: pairwise product-id intersections were computed over all emitted leaves - none is a subset of another.

ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: RULE 3 CHECK - catalogue is LIVE. Swoon Editions went through a pre-pack administration in Dec 2019 (co-founders bought the business back) but continues to trade under the same name; Companies House shows SWOON EDITIONS LTD active, and the site's own sitemap (https://www.swooneditions.com/media/sitemap.xml) carries lastmod dates up to 2026-07-30, with live Klarna checkout, live stock status and a working cart. So this is a real, current catalogue, not a shutdown. ACCESS ROUTE: the storefront is a client-side React SPA (empty #root in the HTML) so plain curl and WebFetch both return only the shell. The site is Magento 2 behind it and exposes an unauthenticated GraphQL endpoint at https://www.swooneditions.com/graphql (POST) - that is the site's own data layer and it answered every query directly with no impersonation, proxy or interstitial work needed. The category tree came from `{categories(pageSize:400,currentPage:N,filters:{})}` paged 3x to cover all 1114 nodes, cross-checked against the sitemap and against the rendered mega-menu (obtained via r.jina.ai, which renders the SPA nav but lazy-loads the product grid). COUNT VERIFICATION (Rule 1): the storefront publishes NO rendered 'N products' header anywhere - there is no count in the mega-menu, on the category landing pages or on the listing grids. So the only available number is the Magento total. The brief flags Magento `categoryList.product_count` as measured-inflated (169 vs 43 live), so I did not use it unchecked: for every row I ran `{products(pageSize:60,filter:{category_id:{eq:"ID"}}){total_count items{...}}}` and enumerated the category to exhaustion. In all 16 categories tested the returned item array length exactly equalled `total_count` (e.g. Mirrors: total_count 26, 26 distinct product items returned), and the Lighting children summed exactly to the Lighting parent (3+1+2+1+0 = 7). `product_count` and the live `products.total_count` also agreed on every node here, so on this store the Magento total survives the exhaustion check. Counts were read twice ~20 min apart with zero drift. SCOPE - Swoon is furniture-led, so the in-scope list is genuinely short, as anticipated. EXCLUDED and why: Sofas, Chairs, Tables, Storage, Beds, Footstools, Desks, Kitchen (cat 201 = kitchen furniture, no tableware or glassware), Bespoke furniture, Outdoor Furniture - all furniture. Rugs (1), Curtains (2), Cushions (0), Bedding (0), and the Piglet throws/napkin sitting loose in Accessories - textiles. 'Speedy lighting' (6) and 'Speedy accessories' (7) - delivery-speed facets, not product categories. Style branches (Art deco, Mid-century, Industrial, Classic, Contemporary, Scandi) including their /lighting and /accessories children - pure style re-cuts, and every one of their lighting nodes is empty (0) anyway. New In, Bestsellers, Clearance, Refurbished, Flash SALE, Collections, Look Book, Editions, Range, Delivery by Christmas, Almost/Not Perfect, Gift cards, Swatches, 'Test & Utility cat' - marketing/nav/internal, never extractable. MIRROR SUB-NODES DROPPED AS PROVEN FACET RE-CUTS: the mega-menu offers Brass mirrors (4), Rectangle mirrors (14), Hexagon mirrors (0) and Round mirrors (4) under Mirrors. I compared product-ID sets rather than names: they sum to 22 against a parent of 26, they overlap each other (lark-luxe-round-mirror-silver-mirror-black-steel appears in BOTH Brass mirrors and Round mirrors), and Round mirrors even contains a non-mirror (harpenden-scandi-floating-dressing-table). They are shape/material filters over the same 26 mirrors, so Mirrors is emitted as a single leaf carrying the authoritative 26.

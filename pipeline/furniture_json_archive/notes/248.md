ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: Access route: direct fetch worked initially (nav, footer sitemap, mega-menu, collection HTML), then the session hit Shopify/Cloudflare's 'Verifying your connection' challenge (HTTP 429) on plain requests/r.jina.ai; switched to curl_cffi with chrome131 TLS impersonation, which bypassed it cleanly for all subsequent /collections/<handle>/products.json calls. Count method: EXACT via Shopify /collections/<handle>/products.json?limit=250, paged and ID-deduped (RULE 1) -- never the collections.json 'products_count' metafield, which is confirmed inflated here (e.g. table-wall-decor metafield=1362 vs enumerated unique products=94, a 14.5x inflation; dining-table-accessories metafield=2126 vs its real children being far smaller). Chumbak is a Shopify store with ~550 collections, the vast majority being sale/discount/gift/price-tier/occasion marketing collections (e.g. '50% Discount', 'Gifts Under 1000', 'Black Friday') -- these were excluded per the Shop-All/Sale/Gift-Guide rule. Category taxonomy used: the real customer-facing mega-menu + footer SEO-link sitemap (Dining group: Dining Table Accessories/Crockery Set/Tableware/Coffee Mugs/Storage Jars/Bakeware; Decor & Living group: Table & Wall Decor/Planters/Trunks & Storage/Rugs & Carpets), plus two orphaned-but-live named collections not in nav (glassware, wall-clocks) that were confirmed real via products.json (non-empty, 200 OK) and match required scope buckets (Glassware, Clocks). Exclusions applied: Storage Jars and Bakeware (kitchen storage/cookware) dropped entirely; Trunks & Storage and Rugs & Carpets dropped (furniture/textiles); within Serving & Tableware, 5 kitchen storage-jar/lunch-box SKUs and 1 souvenir-magnet SKU were dropped via per-SKU product_type+title inspection (129 raw -> 123 in-scope); within Table & Wall Decor, 17 non-decor SKUs were dropped (storage boxes, desk calendars, an organiser gift set, seagrass laundry baskets, a picnic-cutlery basket, bamboo/rattan storage boxes, a dinnerware trinket dish) via per-SKU product_type+title inspection (94 raw -> 77 in-scope), then 4 wall-clock SKUs within that bucket were further subtracted to avoid double-representing the same 4 SKUs already reported under the dedicated Clocks row (77 -> 73 final). Candles collection (15 SKUs) was inspected and dropped entirely: titles are dominated by 'Soy Wax', scent-named candles (Apple & Cinnamon, Citrus & Sweet, Orange Blossom, Pomegranate) i.e. Home Fragrance, which is out of scope, and the collection could not be cleanly split into a genuine unscented-only subset. Rule 0 gains: bobble-head novelty figurines (bobble-heads-figurines collection) counted as Figurines despite the whimsical name; ceramic tea-light holders (found inside the Table & Wall Decor bucket, product_type 'Candles & Home Fragrances') counted as decorative candle holders, not excluded as fragrance, since the holder itself (not a scented candle) is explicitly in scope. Mirrors and Lighting/lamps: confirmed absent, not a false-nav-absence -- checked both by scanning the full ~550-collection handle/title list (zero matches for 'mirror', zero genuine matches for 'lamp'/'light' fixtures; only 'Candles & Light' exists and is a candles collection) and via on-site search (site's own search page reports '6 results for mirror', a real server-rendered count, but Chumbak sells no dedicated mirror product line -- it is a dinnerware/drinkware/bags/fashion-accessory brand with a small decor vertical, consistent with the near-total absence of any mirror or lighting-fixture SKUs across all inspected collections). No duplicate-company concern: Chumbak is a single, unambiguous, well-known Indian D2C brand (Chumbak Design Pvt Ltd), chumbak.com is its sole storefront.

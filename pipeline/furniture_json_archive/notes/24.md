## Prior Home-Decor pass (archive c25) status=ok
ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: Off-price retailer with a genuine online catalogue. www.marshalls.com is Akamai-protected (HTTP 403 / 'Something went wrong' failover to plain requests, WebFetch, r.jina.ai, translate.goog and microlink). Reached the origin with curl_cffi using iOS Safari impersonation profiles (safari18_0_ios / safari184_ios), which serve the mobile origin m.marshalls.com; every mobile page declares its desktop www.marshalls.com canonical URL, and those canonical URLs are what is recorded in 'link'. The Endeca catalogue is shallow: Home has only three levels and no clock, vase, lantern or candle-holder nodes. Excluded as out of scope: Bed & Bath, Bedding, Baskets & Storage, Blankets & Throws, Rugs, Throw Pillows, Curtains, Table & Kitchen Linens, Cookware & Bakeware, Cutlery & Flatware, Cookbooks, Books, Chairs & Seating, Tables, Luggage & Travel, Pet, Stationery, Outdoor Furniture/Rugs/Beach & Pool, and the seasonal Halloween / Fall Decor / Back to Campus / Gifts / Clearance / New Arrivals buckets. 'Candles' (129) was dropped: its assortment is dominated by scented candles and reed diffusers, which this batch excludes as Home Fragrance. 'Shop By Room' and 'Shop by Style' are cross-cutting merchandising roll-ups, not product categories. Marshalls' listing header is the literal 'N Items' string next to the H1; where a node's own header could be loaded it is quoted verbatim. Akamai rate-limits to roughly two full page loads per cooldown window, so some nodes fall back to the itemCount that the site renders into its own navigation JSON inside every PLP document - that number was confirmed identical to the rendered header on every node where both could be read.

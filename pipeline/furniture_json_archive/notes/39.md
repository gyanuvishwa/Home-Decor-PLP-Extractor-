## Prior Home-Decor pass (archive c42) status=ok
ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: Shopify store, reached by direct fetch (no blocking). The collection grid is NOT in the server HTML and the theme renders no 'N items' header and no pagination, so counts came from the Searchspring layer that actually powers the grid (siteId qva9ht, discovered in the theme's ss-collection-snap.js): https://qva9ht.a.searchspring.io/api/search/search.json?siteId=qva9ht&bgfilter.collection_handle=<handle> -> pagination.totalResults. EVERY one of the 29 leaf counts was then independently re-verified by exhaustively paging /collections/<handle>/products.json and counting unique product IDs: 29 of 29 matched exactly, so the structured total is not inflated here. Parent/child sums also reconcile (Ceiling 32 = Chandeliers 29 + Flush Mounts 3; Lamps 51 = Table 37 + Floor 14; All Lighting 106 = 32+51+23; Art 248 = Prints 231 + Beaded 14 + Sculptures 3). All 29 links return HTTP 200 and the leaves are mutually disjoint apart from 2 pendant-style items cross-listed in Decorative Objets. SUBSET/RE-CUT CATEGORIES DROPPED after comparing product-ID sets, not names: the entire POTTERY department is a material re-cut and every one of its sub-categories is 100% contained in the Decor or Dining equivalent (Pottery Vases 53 of 53 inside Decor Vases 71; Trays 36/36; Bowls 17/17; Decorative Objets 3/3; Picture Frames 6/6; Candle Holders 7/7; Serveware & Mugs 99 = all of Pottery Dining); Art > Photography (32) is 100% inside Art > Prints (231); Lighting > Pendants (7) is 100% inside Chandeliers (29); Decor > Jewelry Boxes (11) is 100% inside Decorative Boxes (66); Decor > Wall Art (14) is 100% inside Art (248) so it was kept only once, under Art. All 'View All' roll-ups (lighting-all-*, dining-all-*, art-all-art, all-wall-art, decor-and-pillows-all-decor) are used as hierarchy only and carry no qty; decor-and-pillows-all-decor in particular is a 616-item mixed roll-up whose in-scope children sum to far less, so it was deliberately not emitted as a leaf. Two nav nodes are genuinely empty on both endpoints and were dropped: Dining > Mugs (dining-drinkware-mugs = 0) and Art > Painting (art-by-medium-painting = 0).

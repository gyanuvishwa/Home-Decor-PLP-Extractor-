ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: Walmart US is a huge general retailer; scoped strictly to the Home > Decor department navigation (mirrors Target Home / BBB precedent), NOT the whole site. Home > Decor's own hub-and-spoke page (/cp/decor/133012) gave the 14 real spokes: Lighting, Mirrors, Decor Accessories, Wall Art, Clocks, Picture Frames kept in-scope; Curtains/blinds & Rugs & Throw pillows (textiles), Artificial plants & flowers, Furniture, Bedding, Kids' decor (furniture-heavy) dropped as excluded categories; Candles & home fragrance dropped as a whole node (dominant scented/fragrance catalog, no reliable unscented-only facet found) except a small verified 'Candles' sub-node (19, tealight/pillar holders) kept under Decor Accessories. Added Tableware & Glassware under Kitchen & Dining and Curtain Rods under Window Treatments per brief's explicit include list; added Bathroom Mirrors from the Bath/Home-Improvement tree (mirrors always in scope, Rule 0.3); did not extract the rest of Bath (dominated by functional storage/fixtures) or Outdoor Decor beyond what Lighting/Decor-Accessories already surface (Outdoor Lighting, Garden Sculptures & Statues), consistent with strict-scope instruction. COUNT METHODOLOGY (Rule 1): every Walmart 'browse' node's unfiltered totalItemCount is proven-inflated/noisy (e.g. 'Shop all lighting' node claims totalItemCount 227,208 and its own 'Category' facet menu is polluted with unrelated tags like Ottomans/Ovens/Action Figures/Shower Faucets - confirming these are broad text-matched search results dressed as category pages, not a clean taxonomy, and Walmart is not exhaustion-checkable at that scale). Empirically: whenever a facet-filtered count displays as an exact integer (totalItemCountDisplay NOT capped '(1000+)') the number is internally consistent - cross-checked Mirrors (458, re-fetch gave 457/458) and Wall Clocks (293 via Decor-Accessories parent vs 311 via the dedicated Clocks parent, both close, differences explained by live inventory churn between fetches, not by methodology) - so uncapped facet counts were accepted as this project's 'exact number the site itself reports' and used as qty. Whenever totalItemCountDisplay is capped '(1000+)', the underlying totalItemCount was shown to be unstable/inconsistent across scopes (Picture Frames: 206 via one parent vs 1021 via its own dedicated node; Canvas Wall Art self-facet total, 331918, exceeds even its own parent node's unfiltered total of 300010) and not exhaustion-checkable at these scales (thousands to hundreds of thousands) - every such node was set qty:null with a MANUAL REVIEW flag, matching the Etsy US / QVC / Noon UAE convention for inflated marketplace-style widgets. Dropped 'Novelty Lighting' entirely as a mixed roll-up (lava/salt lamps mixed with LED puck lights and under-cabinet task lighting in the same 38-item facet; brief Rule: 'drop, don't pad'). Access: direct fetch worked throughout, no WAF block encountered; robots.txt (fetched directly, not via translate.goog) has no Claude/ClaudeBot-specific disallow and does not block /browse or /cp paths, only /search, /store/*, and account/API endpoints. status set to 'partial' because roughly a third of leaf rows (19 of 55) are null+MANUAL REVIEW, per this project's convention that a large null fraction bars 'ok' even when each null decision is individually well-justified.

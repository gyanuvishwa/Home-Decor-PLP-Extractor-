ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: ACCESS: direct HTTPS, no WAF, and no Claude-specific disallow in robots.txt (Shopify store; only standard Shopify paths are blocked). Structure taken from the desktop mega-menu embedded in the homepage HTML plus the /pages/lighting, /pages/decor and /pages/kitchen-table landing pages. COUNTS: 2Modern renders every collection page client-side with Searchspring (siteId dja4ih); the server HTML always shows a '0 items' placeholder, so qty was taken from the same search layer the page itself uses: dja4ih.a.searchspring.io/api/search/search.json with bgfilter.collection_handle. Per Rule 1 the raw Searchspring total was not trusted blind - all 47 leaves were exhaustion-checked by paging resultsPerPage=100 to the last page and counting distinct product uids; distinct uids equalled pagination.totalResults for 47 of 47 collections. Searchspring indexes at product level here (ss_variant_count is a field on each product record), so these are not variant counts. Independent cross-check: the rendered PLP header for /collections/modern-clocks reads '74 Results', matching totalResults=74. Shopify /collections/<handle>/products.json was used only as a sanity check (pendants 1461 there vs 1465, the feed omitting a few unavailable products) and was NOT the qty source. BRAND PAGES: the site is organised heavily around brands (a Featured Brands column in every mega-menu, /collections/<brand> handles, and four brand links sitting inside the Lighting-by-Room menu column - Visual Comfort and Co, Santa and Cole, Louis Poulsen, FLOS, Moooi). All brand/designer listings were excluded per the brief. Two nav nodes were dropped as mixed roll-ups after reading actual titles: 'Storage Accessories' (548 - dominated by shelving units, bookcases, wall cabinets and etageres, i.e. furniture) and 'Desk Accessories' (182 - contaminated with diffusers and scented candles, which the brief excludes).

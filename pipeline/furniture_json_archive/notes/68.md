Direct fetch worked throughout (no WAF/blocking encountered). The decoration-accessories page itself (177 items) is a MIXED ROLL-UP of all of the above incl. pillows/throws -- dropped per 'drop roll-ups, do not pad'. Counts verified via two independent signals that agreed: (1) rendered '<N> Products' header text and (2) the page's embedded Algolia InstantSearch result blob (nbHits, hitsPerPage:12, nbPages == ceil(nbHits/12)); each category re-fetched live twice (this session, ~15 min apart) with identical nbHits both times -- no drift observed. /all-lighting/ (Lighting, 19) already aggregates all lamp/lighting sub-types (table-lamps, floor-lamps, ceiling-lamps, pendants carry 'all-lighting' as a co-tag on every product) -- those sub-URLs are facet re-cuts of the same population, not counted separately. /wall-art/ (82) product-title-audited: at least 2 of the sampled ~20 unique titles across two page-1 fetches are literally Wallpaper products ('Green Tropical Jungle Wallpaper', 'Modest Geo Wallpaper') bleeding into this nav bucket (matches the mobile nav's own conflated label 'Wall Decor & Wallpaper') -- flagged as MANUAL REVIEW rather than silently padded or silently dropped, because the site itself reports 82 for this URL and the contamination could not be exactly quantified: pagination beyond page 1 is client-side/AJAX (no working ?page= param, no visible pagination links in SSR HTML) so exhaustive enumeration was not reachable without browser automation, which this project disallows. /wall-decor/ (68) was found via sitemap.xml but is NOT linked anywhere in current site navigation (checked home page mega-menu and mobile flyout); its page-1 product IDs overlap ~83% (10/12) with /wall-art/'s page-1 IDs (e.g. product 11455 'Lucky Bird Wall Art' appears verbatim on both) -- treated as a legacy/duplicate reading of the same population per Rule 0.6 (drop when it's the same listing under a second URL at the same level) and NOT emitted as its own row; the overlap is documented here rather than silently discarded. Five sitemap-listed category pages -- /clocks/, /vases-and-bowls/, /tableware/, /glassware/, /serveware/ -- are live (HTTP 200, real per-category title tags and templates) but are NOT linked anywhere in current site navigation and their embedded Algolia blob returns nbHits:0 (verified live twice each, ~15 min apart, no drift) -- reported as genuine qty:0 rows rather than omitted, since the brief specifically asked to check thoroughly before concluding low/zero. /plants-and-planters/ (1 item, 'Penelope Planter', a rattan/cane pot) is a genuine decorative planter, not a faux plant, so kept in scope; a second sitemap URL /plants-and-planters/plants/ (live plants) was excluded as not a decor object. All '/modern/...' prefixed URLs (e.g. /modern/mirrors/, /modern/all-lighting/) are a style-facet re-cut (products carry a co-tag like 'modernwall-decor') of the same canonical categories and were not counted separately. Excluded per standing rules: Pillows, Throws, Rugs, Poufs & Cubes (textiles/furniture), Wallpaper (dedicated /wallpaper/ page, and the wallpaper bleed inside /wall-art/), Art Prints (subset of Wall Art, no separate listing page), all furniture/bedroom/office/outdoor-furniture/mattress lines.

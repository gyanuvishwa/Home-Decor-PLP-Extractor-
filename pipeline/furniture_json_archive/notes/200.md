ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: No prior project precedent (first Canada-market worker to run this company; not a listed duplicate). Indigo.ca is Shopify-hosted; robots.txt carries only standard Shopify boilerplate disallows (checkout/cart/account/search/policies), no Claude-specific block. Site publishes an agents.md (linked from a dedicated sitemap_agentic_discovery.xml) that explicitly sanctions GET /collections/{handle}/products.json for read-only browsing -- used as the primary, authoritative data source. Indigo is fundamentally a bookstore with a small secondary 'Home' lifestyle department (mega-menu group: Water Bottles, Candles, Mugs, Home Decor, Entertaining) -- no furniture, no mirrors, no dedicated clock product line found anywhere on the site (a /collections/clocks-watches, /collections/lighting, /collections/sculpture and /collections/sculpture-installation all exist but are BOOK genre collections, confirmed by title sampling, not physical product listings -- classic false-friend collection-handle trap). /collections/collectibles-figurines (224 items) is kids' toy blind-boxes/plushies/Pokemon figures, not decorative figurines -- excluded after title review. COUNT-INFLATION CAUGHT: the site's Constructor.io search/browse API (ac.cnstrc.com/browse/group_id/...) reports total_num_results per color-variant, not per product -- e.g. 'entertain-serve' via Constructor browse showed 146 for candles-home-fragrance (only 50 unique Shopify product ids), 156 for mugs (only 116-126 unique depending on pagination drift), 74 for home-decor (60 unique). Abandoned Constructor.io entirely in favour of Shopify's native /collections/{handle}/products.json, which returns exactly one JSON object per product (verified unique count of returned objects == count of distinct Shopify product ids in every case), used as ground truth for every qty below. 3 genuine product collections cover all in-scope decor/tableware: Home Decor (61 raw -> 24 in-scope: Lighting 5, Vases & Planters 12, Bookends 6, Decorative Bowls 1 -- rest is Pillows/Throws (textiles), Seasonal ornaments (Holiday), a candle warmer (Home Fragrance), a toothbrush holder (personal care/bath storage, not decorative), a cookbook holder, a book storage basket and a desk pad, all excluded), Entertain & Serve (116 raw -> 35 in-scope: Glassware & Drinkware 14, Dinnerware 12, Serveware 4, Coasters & Table Accessories 5 -- rest is Gourmet food/tea/chocolate (48), tea towels (6, textile), kitchen tools/bar tools/cutlery (Impulse & Novelty KITCHEN & HOME etc., ~15), stationery/recipe binders, all excluded), and Mugs (126 raw, all 126 in-scope -- genuine coffee mugs/teacups/teapots, filed under Kitchen & Dining per project convention for drinkware/dinnerware). Fully excluded after empirical check: Candles & Home Fragrance (51 unique products, every title is an explicitly scented candle -- e.g. 'Classic Citron Scented Candle', 'Floral Garden Rose Scented Candle' -- none qualify as the brief's unscented-candle exception), Water Bottles (19, travel/sport tumblers e.g. HydroJug/Vista, not tableware), Seasonal Decor (14, Halloween/holiday ornaments and memorial/wedding guest books), Baby & Nursery Decor (14, almost entirely baby memory-books/journals; the 2 marginal picture-frame items -- a sonogram frame and a baby-photo frame -- are keepsake-context items outside the Home/Lifestyle decor department and were left out rather than padding a 1-2-item 'Picture Frames' row). No Rule 0.6 overlap: Home Decor / Entertain & Serve / Mugs are three fully distinct collections with no shared product ids. All qty fully resolved empirically (no nulls needed) -> status ok.

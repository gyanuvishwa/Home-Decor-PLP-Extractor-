ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: Live Shopify storefront (US site of the Danish design brand), direct fetch worked throughout, no WAF/blocking encountered. Real nav taxonomy read from the homepage's mega-menu (data-dynamic-menu-parent/data-dynamic-menu-child attributes), cross-checked against the full collections list (/collections.json, 182 collections). All qty values were verified by fully paginating each collection's own /collections/<handle>/products.json?limit=250 endpoint to exhaustion (looping pages until an empty page is returned) and de-duplicating by unique numeric product ID - this is the strict exhaustion/unique-ID method the brief prefers over any single reported total field. Rule 0 applications: kept 'Kids Lamps' (8) as a Lighting sub-category even though the site's own nav files it under the 'Kids' menu, not 'Lighting' - judged by product type (it is a lamp fixture), not by room/audience. Kept 'Outdoor Pots' (53) as its own row alongside 'Plant Pots' (72) per Rule 0.6: confirmed by title comparison that ~44 SKUs are shared between the two listings (Hourglass Pot, Staffa Planter, Bau Pot, Evoke Pot, Dodu Pot, Rollo Pot, Fragma Planter, Gemi Stackable Planters etc.) while Outdoor Pots also carries about 9 outdoor-exclusive SKUs not in Plant Pots (Bau/Uma Balcony Box, Lapel Planter) - a genuine room-based nav branch overlapping a type branch, both kept, overlap documented rather than dropped. EXCLUDED after inspection: 'Candles' (14: 6 'Dryp Candles' + 4 'Pure Candles', both explicitly described as 'pure stearin' with no scent claim, plus 4 'Vitto Scented Candle' items which are explicitly scented) - the unscented items have no dedicated listing URL of their own (only exist inside the mixed 'candles' collection), so per the brief's 'take unscented children where they exist as their own listing; drop the whole node where they don't' the whole node was dropped rather than fabricating a filtered count. 'Outdoor Accessories' (57) - inspected product titles; the large majority (~35+ of 57) are furniture cushions/seat pads, protective covers for outdoor seating, umbrellas, garden tools and bags; only a minority (door knockers, water globes, a bird feeder, trays, a couple of sculptural pieces) are genuine decor, with no clean sub-listing to isolate them, so dropped as a non-decor-majority mixed roll-up. 'Kids Accessories' (37) - inspected titles; roughly 10 of 37 are textile wall maps, bed canopies, wallpaper, clothes hangers or bags (clear excludes) with no dedicated sub-URL to strip them out, so dropped rather than padding the qty with off-scope items despite the majority being decorative kids-room hooks/planters. 'Bags and Baskets' (16) - mostly literal fashion tote/weekend bags plus laundry baskets/a laundry stand; dropped as non-decor. 'Decorations' (21) and 'Wall Decorations' (19, which contains a genuine 5-colorway 'Melt Wall Clock' line plus pinboards/frame rings mixed with tapestry blankets and wall rugs) - both collections exist in the store's /collections.json but are NOT linked anywhere in the live homepage mega-menu (checked: zero href matches for either handle in the nav HTML), so treated as orphaned/legacy collections outside the site's current real taxonomy rather than as genuine nav categories, per Rule 3's 'establish what the site actually is'. 'Collect Lighting' (51), 'Shades' (36) and 'Sockets' (14) - the site's own Lighting submenu has no entry for any of these three; they are only reachable via the '/pages/collect-lighting' build-your-own-lamp configurator tool. Verified 'Collect Lighting' (51) is an exact facet rollup: Shades (36) + Sockets (14) + 1 shared 'Collect Ring' accessory = 51, confirming it is a pure union of the other two collections rather than a distinct category, so all three were dropped. 'Shelves' (71), 'Storage' (100, generic), 'Small Storage' is the one Accessories-menu storage item and was kept (below) while the broader unmenued 'storage' handle was not used.

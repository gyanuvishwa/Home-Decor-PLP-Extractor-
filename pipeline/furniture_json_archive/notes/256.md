## Prior Home-Decor pass (archive c300) status=ok
ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: Shopify store behind a Cloudflare managed challenge (JS challenge) that blocked plain requests, r.jina.ai, and translate.goog (all 429/challenge HTML). Accessed via curl_cffi Chrome-124 TLS impersonation, which reached the Shopify JSON side-doors (collections.json, /collections/<handle>/products.json) directly (HTTP 200). WebFetch also worked for rendered pages and was used to cross-verify. IMPORTANT: this store's Shopify collection 'products_count' field is inflated versus reality (confirms prior finding: shopify-products-count-is-unreliable) - e.g. collections.json reported wall-accents-frames=200, paintings=275, dining-serveware=139, artefacts=811, but full products.json pagination (limit=250, paged to exhaustion, deduped by product id) gave 153, 56, and 113 respectively. Every qty below is the EXHAUSTION-PAGINATION count, and was cross-checked against the site's own rendered 'N products' collection-page header via WebFetch for a sample spanning every included category (clocks 19=19, paintings 56=56, dining-serveware 113=113, home-accents-lighting 24=24, wall-accents-frames 153=153, dining-tableware 43=43, idols-figurines-brass 167=167, ganesha-brass 31=31, graphic-tableware 119=119) - all matched exactly, so the pagination-exhaustion method is treated as ground truth for the rest. Nav mirrors: Ganesha / Home Decor (Wall Accents, Home Accents, Dining, Cushion Covers, Furniture) / Artefacts (Idols & Figurines by material, Decor) / Graphic Works (Tableware, Drinkware, Bags, Diaries, Cushion Covers, Umbrellas, T-Shirts) / Accessories / Souvenirs / Gifting. EXCLUDED per scope: Home Decor > Cushion Covers and Furniture (furniture/textiles); Home Accents > Small Organizers (storage boxes/trinket boxes/piggy banks - not decor) and Chowki (low wooden stool - furniture); Souvenirs (stationery/toys/dolls/mementos), Accessories (bags/wallets/jewellery/wellness), Graphic Works > Bags/Diaries/Umbrellas/T-Shirts/Cushion Covers (non-decor), Gifting (price-tier bundle listing, not a product category). Graphic Drinkware and Copper Bottles/Pots/Glass were DROPPED as mixed roll-ups/cross-cutting theme filters - product_type inspection showed most of their items already carry the 'Home Decor - Dining - Drinkware' product_type and are already counted under Kitchen & Dining > Drinkware, so including them would double-count. Idols & Figurines 'Masterpieces' sub-collection was DROPPED as a mixed roll-up - title/product_type inspection showed it re-pulls items already tagged into the material-specific collections (Brass, Wood, Bone, etc.) plus unrelated items (e.g. a Wall Accents wood-art-frame appeared inside it), so it is a marketing cross-listing, not a distinct product set. Generic top-level 'shop by material' collections (brass, wood, resin, iron, silver, marble - as opposed to the nav-linked idols-figurines-<material> and ganesha-<material> collections) were DROPPED as cross-cutting filter/browse pages that re-list items from many unrelated categories (frames, storage boxes, stationery, drinkware) - not genuine nav categories, and NEVER-EXTRACT covers filter pages. Mirrors and Glassware: verified TRUE ABSENCE, not a default-search artifact - the site has no dedicated Mirrors or Glassware collection/nav entry; a site search-suggest check for 'mirror' returned only 3 incidental non-mirror-category items (a mirror-work wall hanging, mirror-work cushion, mirror-work wall hanging), and 'glassware' returned 0 results, so this is a genuine gap, not underlogged. Diyas (traditional oil lamps) were classified under Lighting per Rule 0 (product type - a light-emitting fixture - beats the festival/religious association). Company is a well-known standalone Indian heritage lifestyle/gifting retailer (est. 1979); no indication of being a duplicate of any other company in this project's list.

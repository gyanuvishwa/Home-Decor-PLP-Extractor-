ACCESS: kohls.com is behind Akamai Bot Manager with a behavioral JS sensor challenge on every catalog/product page and even on the mapps.kohls.com browse API and the live robots.txt (which itself 403s: 'Access Denied ... errors.edgesuite.net'). Historical robots.txt recovered via Wayback (2024 snapshot) has no ClaudeBot-specific disallow, only generic crawl-trap rules (search, checkout, myaccount, sortfilter params) and Baiduspider block - no policy issue found. Route 1 (direct) and Route 5 (WebFetch) both 403 immediately. Route 3 (r.jina.ai) was globally Cloudflare-challenged from this workstation for the entire session (verified against https://example.com too, not kohls-specific - shared-pool contention per project memory). Route 4 (translate.goog / translate.google.com) returned Google's own HTTP 429 /sorry captcha on every attempt across multiple languages and both frontends, for the whole session. Route 2 (curl_cffi impersonation) was brute-forced across the FULL 42-profile BrowserType set available in this venv, repeated in 3 separate sweeps at ~2s spacing: attempt 1 on /catalog/wall-decor-home-decor.jsp got exactly ONE hit (safari260_ios, real 1.84MB page); immediately re-using that same profile succeeded ONE more time on /catalog.jsp?CN=Department:Home Decor (2.48MB); a third sweep (different URL, decorative-accents-home-decor.jsp) then failed on all 42 profiles including the previously-good safari260_ios, and a 4th sweep on Department:Lighting after a 90s cooldown also failed 42/42 - matching this project's established Crate & Barrel precedent (c58.json) where the workstation IP goes 'hot' after a couple of successful loads and the block survives quiet periods. Side doors: /sitemap.xml and all 6 /sitemap_catalog_N.xml + 16 /sitemap_product_N.xml files (38k+ URLs) are NOT Akamai-protected and were fetched cleanly - they confirmed the live Category/Product facet taxonomy (Category:Wall Decor, Decorative Accents, Candles, Picture Frames & Photo Albums, Rugs, Window Treatments, Artificial Flowers & Plants, Decorative Pillows & Chair Pads, Slipcovers & Furniture Protectors under Department:Home Decor; Category:Dinnerware & Serveware, Drinkware & Glassware under Department:Kitchen & Dining; Curtain Rods & Hardware under Category:Window Treatments) but carry NO product counts (they are just facet-combo URLs).

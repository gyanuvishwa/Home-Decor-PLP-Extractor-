## Prior Home-Decor pass (archive c64) status=ok
ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: Live Shopify storefront, reached via direct fetch (no blocking encountered). Full mega-menu nav scraped from homepage HTML (same header on every page). qty is EXACT unique-product-ID count from exhaustive pagination of https://www.heals.com/collections/<handle>/products.json (limit=250, paged to empty page), NOT the /collections.json aggregate 'products_count' field, which was proven inflated here (e.g. ceiling-lights 316 vs 285 actual unique IDs; mirrors 86 vs 72 actual) -- consistent with the brief's Shopify warning. HIERARCHY METHOD: for each type-tree group (Ceiling Lights, Table Lamps, Lampshades, Wall Lights, Floor Lamps, Mirrors, Glassware, Dinnerware) I verified by product-ID containment whether the named children exhaustively cover the parent umbrella. Where children's union covered >=90% of the parent (Ceiling Lights ~101%, Lampshades 100%, Mirrors 94%) I used a parent grouping row (qty null) with only the children as leaves, per the standard contract. Where children covered far less of the parent (Table Lamps 40%, Floor Lamps 42%, Wall Lights 82%, Glassware 85%, Dinnerware 4%) the umbrella page contains a large population of real, in-scope products not captured by any narrower nav child, so I kept the umbrella itself as its own leaf row (own verified qty/link) alongside its narrower children as sibling leaves, and documented the subset relationship in each row's evidence -- this follows Rule 0.6's principle that a documented overlap beats a missing/undercounted category, since dropping these to null-qty parents would have silently discarded thousands of real product listings. RULE 0 GAINS: kept Bathroom Wall Lights, Outdoor Lighting, Kitchen Lighting and Bedside Lamps -- all room-based lighting nodes scattered across the Bathroom/Garden/Kitchen/Bedroom nav sections -- which a room-word exclusion would have dropped; Bedside Lamps (137) verified 136/137 as a subset of Table Lamps and is documented as overlapping, not deduplicated away. EXCLUDED: Bulbs & Fittings / Light Bulbs (bulbs/wiring hardware); Wallpaper & Paint (wallpaper + paint, neither in scope); Desk Accessories (mixed roll-up -- verified by titles as predominantly non-decor desk organisers/chargers/notebooks, its few clocks/bookends are incidental and clocks already covered by the dedicated Clocks node); Cutlery and Placemats & Coasters / Table Linen & Settings (verified by titles as majority fabric table linens -- napkins, placemats, tablecloths, runners -- with coasters inseparable into their own URL); Bathroom Storage / Wall Cabinets / Accessories / Face & Body (verified by titles as toilet brushes, bins, soap dispensers, wash bags -- hardware and personal care; NOTE bathroom-accessories contained 3 mirror products -- 'Look Magnify Mirror', 'Magnify Mirror', 'Look Mirror White Oiled Oak' -- not present in the dedicated Mirrors collection and with no separable URL, so they could not be captured as a verifiable row); Home Fragrance / Scented Candles / Room Diffusers / Diffuser Refills (fragrance excluded per brief; Dinner & Pillar Candles kept as the verified unscented child); Kitchenware parent (cookware/tools) except its Jugs & Pitchers child (kept, verified decorative serveware); all furniture, rugs, bedding, throws, cushions, gifts, travel/kids/pet accessories, books & stationery, and clearance/offers navigation.

Extracted surface = Williams Sonoma Home's Lighting and Art & Decor departments, the Tabletop & Bar dinnerware/serveware/glassware tree, the decorative children of Outdoor, and one decor node inside Home Essentials. QTY SOURCE: every qty is the PLP's own rendered 'N Items' header, read 2026-08-08. RULE 0 / ROOM-vs-TYPE OVERLAP: Williams Sonoma Home's Lighting menu has an explicit 'Shop by Category' branch AND an explicit 'Lighting by Room' branch. Both are emitted in full under their own parent rows. The room branch (Living Room 65, Kitchen 59, Dining Room 71, Bedroom 68, Entryway 77, Outdoor 7 = 347 rows of overlapping population against a 176-item department) is a re-cut of the same catalogue by room, but per Rule 0.6 it is a genuine second nav branch, not a colour/size facet, so it is kept and the overlap is stated here and flagged on each row. Kitchen Lighting and Outdoor Lighting were kept on product type, not room name. Two separate Outdoor Lighting nodes exist (Lighting dept 7 items, Outdoor dept 9 items, only 3 shared) and both are kept - they must not be merged on name at the merge step. CANDLES: both candle nodes were checked against actual product titles before being taken. They share 20 products with each other; both kept and documented. The real fragrance node, Home Essentials > 'Candles, Diffusers & Room Sprays' (/shop/homekeeping/candles-fragrance/), was excluded.

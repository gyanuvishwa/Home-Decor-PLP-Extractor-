## Prior Home-Decor pass (archive c100) status=ok
ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: Access route: direct fetch of ikea.com/gb/en/ category pages worked (no blocking encountered); category tree built from the site's own mega-menu / sidebar nav links captured across many category pages (lighting, mirrors, decoration, wall-decor, candles, kitchenware & tableware, flower pots) plus targeted probe.py nav calls to expand deeper sub-nav (Glassware, Dinnerware, Vases & bowls, Clocks, Coffee & tea, Napkins). Product counts and evidence: IKEA's own internal search backend (sik.search.blue.cdtapps.com/gb/en/search, POST with searchParameters.type=CATEGORY and a components[0].component=PRIMARY_AREA block) returns results[0].metadata.max as the category's product total. Verified empirically per Rule 1 for EVERY leaf row: (1) fetched the full window (size=999) and confirmed unique product itemNo count equals metadata.max (exhaustion by unique-ID, not href/tile counting); (2) boundary-tested offset=max-1 (returned exactly 1 item) and offset=max (returned 0 items) confirming max is neither inflated nor truncated. All 57 leaf categories passed both checks cleanly with no discrepancies observed, so metadata.max was used directly as qty. Country/currency: GBP prices and /gb/en/ locale confirm this is the genuine UK storefront. Excluded per standing rules: LED light bulbs, wireless smart light bulbs, and lamp Shades/bases/cords (parts/hardware, not fixtures); Scented candles and Home fragrance (fragrance exclusion); Candle accessories (candle snuffers/lid, functional tool, not a holder); Christmas decorations (holiday); Noticeboards (office/organisation); Artificial plants & flowers and Plants & flowers / dried plants (living or faux botanicals, not decor objects; only empty decorative Flower pots & planters were kept); Mirror cabinets (storage furniture with a mirror door, primary purpose is bathroom storage, not a decorative mirror); Serving cutlery, Cutlery, Cookware, Bakeware, Knives & chopping boards, Kitchen utensils, Kitchen/table textiles, Dishwashing accessories, Napkins (textile), Vacuum flasks, Coffee makers & accessories, Drinking accessories (corkscrews/straws/coasters = bar tools) -- all functional/appliance/textile, out of scope. All *-series pages (e.g. ARSTID, HEKTAR, KORKEN, IKEA 365+) are collection/marketing pages, not categories, and were skipped per the standing 'Collections' exclusion. Rule 0 applied: Bathroom Lighting, Outdoor Lighting, Bathroom Mirrors and Vanity Mirrors were all INCLUDED as room-qualified branches of the type trees (Lighting, Mirrors) that they belong to -- these would have been wrongly dropped under the old room-word exclusion. Rule 0.6 overlap: IKEA's Mirrors department presents both a shape/type cross-cut (Large/Wall/Round/Decorative/Standing mirrors) and a room/use cross-cut (Vanity/Makeup/Bathroom mirrors, Mirrors with lights) as genuinely separate site-curated category IDs (not URL filter facets) -- both trees emitted in full per Rule 0.6, overlap documented here rather than dropped. Lighting similarly mixes a fixture-type tree (under Lamps: Floor/Table/Ceiling/Wall/Pendant/Desk/Spotlights/Smart/Rechargeable/LED lights) with a room/use tree (Bathroom/Outdoor/Decorative/Smart/Integrated lighting) at the same level under the Lighting hub; both kept, overlap expected (e.g. a bathroom wall light counts in both Bathroom Lighting and Wall Lights). Mugs & Cups, Tea Pots & Accessories and Napkin Holders were pulled out of their respective mixed parent roll-ups (Coffee & Tea; Napkins & Napkin Holders) as their own clean leaf categories per the mixed-rollup rule -- the appliance/textile siblings (Vacuum flasks, Coffee makers, Napkins) were dropped, not padded in.

EXCLUSIONS applied: Ceiling Fans, Light Bulbs (lighting-adjacent hardware); Wall Shelves, Wallpaper, Floor & Wall Tile, Curtains & Drapes (textiles), Wreaths & Garlands and 'Botanicals' cat (faux flowers/plants/garlands roll-up -- Vases and Indoor Planters pulled out separately since they're the only in-scope items in that bucket); 'Organizational Decor' and 'Decorative Baskets & Boxes' cat totals dropped as mixed roll-ups (storage bins/laundry baskets filed under /bathroom/sb0/baskets-bins-boxes, confirmed via product titles like 'brown laundry basket organizer', 'seagrass storage baskets' -- pure storage, not decor) though 'Decorative Boxes' sb0 (29, genuinely decorative per Wayfair-family precedent) was kept as its own leaf; 'Napkins, Placemats & Chargers' (116) dropped as a mixed roll-up after title inspection showed it is ~90%+ napkins/placemats (textiles) with only a handful of charger items mixed in and no way to isolate chargers alone; all kitchen tools/appliances/cutlery/cookware/food storage/linens excluded per brief.

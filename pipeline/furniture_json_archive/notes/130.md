ACCESS/METHOD NOTES: Access route: direct fetch worked throughout (no WAF/interstitial encountered); robots.txt has no ClaudeBot-specific disallow, only standard SEO-noise blocking (basket/checkout/search/facet URLs) which does not apply to the category URLs used here. Castorama is a large French DIY/home-improvement chain (Kingfisher/ADEO-family); the vast majority of its catalogue (hardware, building materials, tools, plumbing, garden/DIY, paint, flooring, furniture) was excluded per brief. Genuine decor sub-tree found and extracted in full: Lighting (Luminaire et eclairage, indoor+outdoor, every fixture branch per Rule 0.2), Mirrors (all types per Rule 0.3, including bathroom/illuminated/magnifying variants per Rule 0.4), Wall decor (photo frames, framed pictures, canvas art, posters, clocks, decorative stickers, decorative letters), Home accessories (decorative wall objects, statues, vases, candle holders, bookends, trays, curtain rods/rails/rings - curtain rods explicitly in-scope per brief's 'decorative metal' bullet despite living under the site's 'Decoration textile' folder), and Outdoor decor (garden statues/gnomes/decorative balls/fountains/wind chimes/decorative bells) found via the 'Decoration de jardin' hub. Counts verified via each leaf page's own rendered '<N> produits' results header (BeautifulSoup element-boundary text extraction), reading the literal number including the site's narrow-no-break-space (U+202F) thousands separator - IMPORTANT: a naive whitespace-collapsing regex (as used by the default probe.py count command) silently truncates any 4-digit French-formatted count to its last 3 digits (e.g. it reported 'Ceiling Lights' as 682 when the page actually renders '8 682 produits' with U+202F between the two groups); this was caught and corrected by re-parsing raw text preserving U+202F/NBSP as digit-group separators while treating plain ASCII spaces as token boundaries (verified against several pages by inspecting the unicode-escaped raw text directly). All 4-digit-plus counts below were re-verified this way. Pages rendering the generic capped label 'Plus de 10 000 produits' (Pendant Lights and Chandeliers, Canvas Wall Art, Posters and Framed Prints, Decorative Wall Stickers and Decals) have no exact total available and were left null with a MANUAL REVIEW flag per Rule 1. Four room-scoped lighting hub pages (Living Room, Dining Room, Kitchen, Bedroom Lighting) are curated landing pages with real product tiles but no results/filter header or published count at all (confirmed by absence of the 'Comparer/Filtrer' facet-panel block present on every other leaf) - left null with a flag; they were still extracted as rows per Rule 0.1's requirement to cover every Lighting-by-Room node. EXCLUDED as facet/power-source or feature re-cuts of the type tree (Rule 0.6): 'Eclairage solaire' (solar-powered cross-cut spanning floodlights/bollards/spots/lanterns - same products already counted under their own type node) and 'Luminaire et eclairage connectes' (smart/connected cross-cut dominated by bulbs, hubs, sensors and accessories per product-title inspection, with any genuine smart fixtures already counted under their own type node). EXCLUDED as material/shape/room facet landing pages (site's own '-dyn.cat' URLs, e.g. Grand miroir, Miroir industriel, Suspension interieure metal, Plafonnier dore, Affiche nature): these return the identical population as their parent leaf under a colour/material/shape/room filter. EXCLUDED under the brief's standing rules: light bulbs (Ampoule branch), ceiling fans, wiring/electrical hardware (cable, waterproof sockets, electrical panels, motion detectors, lighting-assembly components under 'Creer son luminaire'), wallpaper/wall-covering/paint/moulding, door/furniture handles and knobs, wall shelving (storage furniture), scented candles and home fragrance (kept only the standalone 'Bougeoir'/candle-holder node, dropped the mixed 'Bougie et senteur' node), dried flowers and artificial plants, curtains/blinds/
